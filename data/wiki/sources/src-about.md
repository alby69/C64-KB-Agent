### About Dustlayer

Dustlayer.com is a blog focused on topics around programming the Commodore C64. The content is aimed to beginners to 6502 code yet people who have a good understanding of programming in another language.

Dustlayer is also the name of the [group](http://csdb.dk/group/?id=7516) founded by [myself](http://csdb.dk/scener/?id=25093) to gather people who want to share their coding knowledge to keep the fascination for the C64 alive for generations to come.
