# Elite over Econet on the Acorn Archimedes

## Joining multiplayer scoreboards from the RISC OS version of Elite

If you are playing Archimedes Elite and want to send scores to a multiplayer scoreboard over Econet, then you only need two things: version 1.14 of Archimedes Elite and the EliteNet application. You can find download links for both of these on the [installing Elite over Econet](https://elite.bbcelite.com/hacks/elite_over_econet/installing.html) page.
